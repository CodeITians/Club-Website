FAQ'S

Are there any membership fees?

No, CodeIT club membership is free for all MCKVIE students.

What are the monthly online events?

We host various online coding events each month to engage and challenge club members.

How can I participate in the events?

Simply stay connected with the club for event announcements and follow the registration process.

Can I showcase my coding projects?

Absolutely! We encourage members to share and discuss their coding projects with the community.

How can I ask a coding question?

You can post your coding doubts in the club's online forum or discussion platform.

Is there a minimum skill level required?

No, we welcome coders of all skill levels, from beginners to experts.

Are there any rewards for participating in events?

Yes, we offer certificates of participation and recognition for outstanding performances.

How can I stay updated about club activities?

Regularly check the club's online platform and keep an eye out for email updates.

Can I suggest topics for upcoming events?

Absolutely! We value member input and consider suggestions for event topics.

Are there mentoring sessions for beginners?

Yes, we conduct mentoring sessions to help beginners grasp coding concepts.

Should I need to be a member to participate in events?

No, you can participate without being a member.Share the club's details and platform links with your friends to encourage them to join.

How will we get the certificates of participation of the events?

In general, we will publish certificates in a Google drive link which will be shared to that event specified created WhatsApp group. That link will be valid for 1 month.

How can I be a member of this club?

It's very easy. Just sign up on our website. And done!

Can any stream student be a Core Member of this club?

No. As this club is specially dedicated to IT Department students, Core members will be selected from IT Department students only.

How can I be a core member of this club?

Everyone will get the opportunity to be a Core Member of this club in their Third Year. But for that, you just have to be a general member of this club previously.

Where will I contact if I have a problem or doubt that is not available in FAQs list?

Just post problem in the "Contact Us" section of the Community page. Our Core members will reach you out.
